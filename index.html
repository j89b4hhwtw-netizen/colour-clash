A quick table talk.

Match the colour or symbol of the top card. Be the first to empty your hand.

  • Skip misses the next player. Reverse changes direction (acts as Skip with two players).
  • Regular: draw one, then play that drawn card or pass. +2 / +4 draws skip the recipient. No penalty stacking. Wild +4 requires having no cards of the current colour.
  • No Mercy: draw until a playable card, then play it. Stack an equal-or-higher draw card, or take the full penalty and miss your turn. The optional 25-card elimination rule applies only if the host enables it.
  • No Mercy extras: 7 swaps hands; 0 passes everyone's hands; Discard All removes that colour; Skip All gives you another turn. There are coloured +4, Wild Reverse +4, +6, +10 and Colour Roulette cards.
  • Roulette: the next player chooses a colour and draws until it appears, then misses their turn. Wild cards do not match.
  • Jump-in, if on: play an exact colour-and-symbol match out of turn. Play continues from you. Not available while a draw stack or roulette is pending.
  • Double play, if on: tick Play two, then select one of two identical cards. Draw amounts add; other effects happen once. After drawing, you can play only the drawn card.
  • Tick Call ONE CARD before leaving one card in the hand you are playing. Forget and you automatically draw two. Swap/pass effects do not trigger extra calls.
  • Starting order: the host arranges the numbered list in the lobby. Player 1 starts. Jump-ins continue from the jumper; reverses, skips and eliminated players affect the next turn.
  • Forced pickup: taking a draw-card penalty ends your turn. A normal single draw in Regular may be played immediately if legal.
  • Each turn lasts two minutes. On timeout, you take any pending penalty, or draw one if you have not drawn, then pass. Roulette timeout chooses red.

House-rule adaptation for 2+ players, using two decks. Finishing your hand wins immediately, before its final action. UNO calls are automatic penalties; there are no challenges or points. Online connection required.
